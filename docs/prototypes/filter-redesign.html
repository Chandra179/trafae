What changed in this prototype

  • The language, rating and year controls move out of the hero and into a results toolbar, next to the result count. They only appear once there is something to filter.
  • Each filter is one labeled dropdown that always shows its current value. The year filter offers plain-language presets plus an optional custom range.
  • Active filters appear as removable chips, with a Clear all action.
  • Failed catalogs collapse into one quiet line with details on click, instead of three red chips.
  • The Read now button uses the brand green, matching the primary search action.
